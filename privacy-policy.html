SkyFrameSMP — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

SkyFrameSMP respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it may be shared when you access our website, Minecraft server, online store, or related services.

By using our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect or process the following information.

Website Information

When visiting our website, certain technical information may be collected, including:

  • IP addresses.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages requested.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Basic connection and diagnostic information.

This information is collected automatically through our hosting provider, infrastructure, or server logs.

Minecraft Server Information

When connecting to SkyFrameSMP, we may process game-related information, including:

  • Minecraft username.
  • Player UUID, where available.
  • IP address and connection information.
  • Playtime and gameplay statistics.
  • In-game purchases and virtual balances.
  • Moderation records, including punishments.
  • Other information necessary to operate the server.

This information helps us operate the server, maintain security, provide gameplay features, and enforce our community rules.

Store Information

When using our online store, we may process:

  • Minecraft username.
  • Products selected or purchased.
  • Transaction identifiers and purchase status.
  • Voucher or redemption information, where applicable.
  • Information provided during checkout.

Payment information is handled by the payment provider used during checkout. We do not intentionally collect or store full payment card details ourselves.

Please consult the payment provider's privacy policy for information about its handling of payment data.

Discord Information

When interacting with our Discord community, information may be processed through Discord and its associated services.

This may include your Discord username, user ID, messages, support requests, and moderation records.

Discord operates independently of SkyFrameSMP and has its own Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain SkyFrameSMP.
  • Provide player statistics and gameplay features.
  • Process purchases and deliver digital products.
  • Maintain server security and prevent abuse.
  • Investigate rule violations and enforce punishments.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve website and server performance.
  • Maintain records necessary for legitimate operational purposes.

We do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for operating our services.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use browser storage technologies, including cookies and local storage, to support website functionality.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember shopping cart contents.
  • Preserve certain website preferences.
  • Improve the browsing experience.
  • Support essential website functionality.

You may clear or restrict browser storage through your browser settings. Doing so may affect certain website features.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate SkyFrameSMP, including:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Cloudflare and network security providers.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Discord and community management services.
  • Other technical providers supporting our services.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or valid legal requests.
  • Investigate security incidents.
  • Prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of SkyFrameSMP and its users.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Github Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Different types of information may have different retention periods.

For example:

  • Server logs may be retained for security and troubleshooting.
  • Player statistics may be retained while the server operates.
  • Purchase records may be retained for transaction management.
  • Moderation records may be retained to enforce community rules.

Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through our services.

These measures may include access controls, network security, infrastructure protections, and other safeguards.

However, no website, server, or online service can guarantee absolute security.

We cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, disclosed, altered, or lost through unauthorized means.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our services may contain links to external websites, including Discord, and Minecraft-related services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

These may include:

  • Requesting access to information associated with you.
  • Requesting corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of certain information.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing.
  • Requesting information about how your data is used.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate security, transaction, moderation, or legal purposes.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us through our official Discord community.

9. Children's Privacy

SkyFrameSMP is a gaming community that may be accessed by younger audiences.

We do not intentionally request unnecessary sensitive personal information from children.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, home addresses, financial details, or other sensitive personal information through our website, Minecraft server, or Discord community.

Parents or guardians who have concerns about information associated with a child may contact us through our official support channels.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, infrastructure, or data practices change.

Any changes will be published on our official website with an updated revision date.

We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
